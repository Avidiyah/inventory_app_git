Import / Export

Work orders come from the CSV export — upload the latest one. Existing numbers are updated in place, so nothing is duplicated and edits made here survive a re-import. Closed work orders are counted and ignored. For Client writes the billing sheet: work order number, material and labor totals, and the full receipt. The operational, re-importable CSV export is next to the Work Orders filters below.

Work Orders

Work orders start Created, become Assigned when one or more technicians are selected, and move to In-Progress when material or labor is added or a supervisor selects it in Edit details. Assigned work can also be started from Scan / Stock. Supervisors manage assignment, status, and labor; Admins can also correct imported details and archive a work order from any status. Technicians can append timestamped Notes and add items. Labor is billed in rounded-up 30-minute increments.
